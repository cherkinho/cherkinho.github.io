About Me

My academic and personal mindset revolves around rigorous analysis. I firmly believe that solving today's multifaceted challenges requires a plurality of skills, merging quantitative logic with human-centric management.

My Academic Journey

Bachelor's Degree

My undergraduate years provided a highly demanding multidisciplinary foundation. Courses ranging from Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry to Statistics pushed me to develop a strictly rigorous analytical mindset. One of my proudest achievements during this time stemmed from an introductory programming course: leveraging what I learned, I took the initiative to code and develop two fully functional applications on my own. Master's Degree (M1)

It serves as an accurate simulation of the real workplace—teaching me how to navigate team dynamics, distribute workloads, and collaborate efficiently with different personalities to achieve a common goal.
