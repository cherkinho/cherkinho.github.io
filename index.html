About Me

Driven by a deep passion for optimization and a constant curiosity to learn, I am always looking for ways to make complex systems more efficient. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Business Engineering and am currently pursuing my Master’s with a major in Supply Chain Management.

My academic and personal mindset revolves around rigorous analysis. I firmly believe that solving today's multifaceted challenges requires a plurality of skills, merging quantitative logic with human-centric management.

My Academic Journey

Bachelor's Degree

My undergraduate years provided a highly demanding multidisciplinary foundation. Courses ranging from Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry to Programming and Statistics pushed me to develop a strictly rigorous analytical mindset. At the same time, subjects like Philosophy, Human Management, and Production & Operations Management bridged the gap between hard science and business realities. The biggest takeaway from my Bachelor's was realizing that top-tier performance in any domain requires mastering a diverse, interconnected skill set.

Master's Degree (M1)

Currently in my Master’s, I am thoroughly enjoying my Major in Supply Chain Management. The program heavily emphasizes group projects, which has introduced an entirely new layer of learning. It serves as an accurate simulation of the real workplace—teaching me how to navigate team dynamics, distribute workloads, and collaborate efficiently with different personalities to achieve a common goal.

Thesis & Academic Interests

Building on my courses in Supply Chain and Production & Operations Management, I am gearing my future Master's thesis toward the field of optimization. While the exact research question is still being defined, I am highly interested in applying supply chain optimization principles to the sports industry—investigating how data and operational efficiency can enhance performance and logistics in a sports context.

Future Professional Life

Looking ahead, I am aiming for a career dedicated to operational excellence.
